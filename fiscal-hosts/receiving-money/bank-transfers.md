# Bank Transfers

Hosts can set up manual bank transfers in order to receive money directly in their bank accounts (via bank transfer).&#x20;

You can enable manual bank transfers by completing the following instructions:

1. Navigate to your Fiscal Host Dashboard > Settings > Receiving Money
2. Under Manual Payments > Bank Transfers, click “Add bank details”
3. Follow the directions on the page to add a **Bank Account Name**, your bank account details and the instructions that will be emailed to contributors when they initiate a manual bank payment.&#x20;

Financial Contributors will then be able to select this bank transfer option as a payment method. It is listed under the Bank Account Name you entered (for example, "Bank Transfer (EUR)"). After completing the donation, they will automatically be emailed the instructions you specified outlining how to manually complete the transaction, this includes a unique transaction identifier (order reference).

They then proceed to make the payment, using the code as a reference.

### EPC QR code (EUR SEPA)

When your bank account has **IBAN and BIC** configured and the contribution is in **EUR**, contributors also see a **Scan to pay** EPC QR code on the contribution success page. The emailed instructions contain the text instructions only.

The QR code encodes payee, IBAN, BIC, amount, and order reference for compatible European banking apps. Scanning pre-fills a SEPA credit transfer in many mobile banking apps.

EPC QR codes appear only for EUR transfers when IBAN and BIC are present. Other currencies or incomplete bank details show text instructions only.

This contribution shows up in your Dashboard under **Incoming Money > Incomplete Contributions**, with the **Pending** status. It shows the amount, the contributor, and the unique ID code (order#).

Once the money has been received in the Host's bank account, an admin of the Host will be able to match the transactions and click **Add received funds** (or **Mark as completed** on the contribution page). The funds will be added to the collective's budget and the contributor will automatically receive a receipt by email.&#x20;

If a pending transfer is cancelled, click **Expire** (or **Mark as expired** on the contribution page) to mark it as expired. Otherwise it will automatically expire two months after it was created. Expired contributions can still be found under the **Expired** tab of Incomplete Contributions. <br>
