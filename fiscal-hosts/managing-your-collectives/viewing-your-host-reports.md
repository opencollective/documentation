# Viewing your Host Reports

It's important to have a good sense of how your Fiscal Host is performing over time.

This is why we offer a Reports page that gives you a clear picture and helps you plan ahead.

{% hint style="info" %}
To find your host's Reports page:

1. Make sure you are switched to your Fiscal Host Admin account
2. Go to your Fiscal Host Dashboard > Reports, and choose **Transactions**, **Expenses** or **Contributions**
{% endhint %}

Fiscal host **accountants** can also view the Transactions, Expenses, and Contributions reports. They must be assigned the Accountant role on the fiscal host.

Each report is available per month, quarter or year. Open a period to see its details. Use the arrows or the period selector at the top of a report to move to another period.

Every report is split into two tabs:

* **Managed funds** (or Managed expenses / Managed contributions): money going to and from your hosted Collectives
* **Operational funds** (or Operational expenses / Operational contributions): money going to and from your Fiscal Host's own account

Your Reports pages will allow you to keep track of:

* **Transactions:** the starting balance, net change and ending balance for the period, broken down by type of transaction, such as contributions, added funds, paid expenses, payment processor fees and collected Host Fees
* **Expenses:** the paid expenses submitted during the period, grouped by accounting category
* **Contributions:** the contributions and added funds received during the period, grouped by accounting category

#### **Exporting your reports**

If you wish to export your data for use elsewhere, open a transactions report and click the "Export" button at the top of the page. You can export all transactions, only managed funds transactions or only operational transactions for that period. You can also export the transactions of a single line of the report from the menu at the end of that line.
