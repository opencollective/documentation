# Zero Collective Balance

Before [changing hosts](../changing-fiscal-host.md) or [closing a Collective](./), the balance must be at zero.

You can move money out of your Collective in several ways:

* [Submitting expenses](../../expenses-and-getting-paid/submitting-expenses/) to withdraw funds.
* [Donating to another Collective](../../giving-to-collectives/giving-to-other-collectives.md) in the same Fiscal Host.
* Giving the remaining balance to your Fiscal Host, if your Fiscal Host supports it. You can access that option by navigating to your Collective's Dashboard > Settings > Advanced.

### Leftover Balance <a href="#leftover-balance" id="leftover-balance"></a>

**Let the Payee cover the fees**

When emptying the balance for a Collective, you have the option of charging the payment processor fees to the payee, which will guarantee that 100% of the Collective's balance will go out. This option is available when the expense is in the same currency as the Collective and is paid by bank transfer (through Wise or manually) or with the "Other" payout method. For manual payments, it only shows once a payment processor fee is entered.

When these conditions are met, the host admins will see a “The payee is covering the fees” checkbox on the Pay expense modal.

**Dealing with small leftovers**

When it's not possible to use the feature previously described, you may sometimes end up with a small amount left behind if you try to withdraw 100% of your balance. This is usually caused by fees being hard to predict exactly.

If this happens, go to your Collective's Dashboard > Settings > Advanced and, in the “Empty Collective balance” section, click “Send \[amount] to \[your Fiscal Host]” to transfer the remaining funds to your Fiscal Host.
