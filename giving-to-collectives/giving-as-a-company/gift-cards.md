# Gift Cards (Discontinued)

Gift cards can no longer be created or claimed.

What still works:

* Already-issued gift cards remain usable until their balance runs out or they expire.
* Recurring contributions paid with a gift card keep working.
* Historical gift card transactions remain visible on Collectives, Organizations, and in the ledger.
