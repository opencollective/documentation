# Changing Organization Currency

* If your Organization does not manage money on the platform (no money management or fiscal hosting), you can change its currency yourself from **Dashboard > Settings > Info**.
* Fiscal Hosts and Organizations with money management can't edit their currency in the settings. Contact Open Collective to change the currency of your Fiscal Host or Organization.
* Collectives must have the same currency as their Fiscal Host.&#x20;
* If your Fiscal Host or Organization has no transactions yet, connecting a Stripe account from **Dashboard > Settings > Receiving Money** automatically changes its currency to the default currency of that Stripe account, so make sure the currency is correct within Stripe before connecting it. Hosted Collectives that have no transactions are updated to the new currency too.
* If your Fiscal Host or Organization already has transactions, the currency can't be changed by reconnecting Stripe or by turning fiscal hosting off and on again. [Contact Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/contact) for help.
