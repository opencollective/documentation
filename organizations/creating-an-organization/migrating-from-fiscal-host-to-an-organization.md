# Migrating from Fiscal Host to an Organization

If you are currently a hosted Collective and want to become independent, follow the steps below.

Remember that once you go independent, you will no longer have the support of a host with your finances and other admin, and you will need your own bank account and legal status.

{% hint style="warning" %}
You will not be able to move your Collective until you have restored your balance to zero.

To find out how to do this, see [Zero Collective Balance](../../collectives/closing-a-collective/zero-collective-balance.md).
{% endhint %}

### To migrate from a Fiscal Host:

1. Ensure that your Collective's balance is empty. See [Zero Collective Balance](../../collectives/closing-a-collective/zero-collective-balance.md) for options.
2. Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Fiscal Host**, click **Leave Host**, and confirm with **Leave [host name]**. If your Collective has active recurring contributions, you will first be asked whether to pause or cancel them.
3. Convert your Collective to an Organization:
   * From **Dashboard > Settings > Fiscal Host**, click **Convert this account to an Organization**, or
   * Go to **Dashboard > Settings > Advanced**, open the **Convert to Organization** section, and click **Convert to Organization**.
4. Enter your Organization's legal name. This should match the legal name on your bank account (check **Dashboard > Settings > Info** if needed). You do not need to change your display name.
5. Confirm the conversion. If you have two-factor authentication enabled, you will be prompted for a 2FA code.
6. Connect your bank account and Stripe account in the onboarding screen.

For general requirements and the reverse conversion (Organization back to Collective), see [Converting between Collectives and Organizations](README.md#converting-between-collectives-and-organizations).

{% hint style="warning" %}
When you leave your Fiscal Host, choose to **pause** your recurring contributions if you plan to keep receiving them as an Organization. Your contributors are notified that their contributions are paused. If you choose to cancel them instead, your contributors are notified that their recurring contributions have been canceled, and they will not be part of the resuming process below.

Once you have converted and connected Stripe, go to **Dashboard > Incoming Money > Incoming Contributions** and use the banner about paused contributions to start the resuming process. Your contributors will receive an email with a link to resume their contribution, followed by a few reminders. Contributions that are not resumed will expire after a few months.
{% endhint %}

### To restore your balance:

If you want to restore a balance you previously emptied, you should:

1. Go to your Dashboard > Accounts
2. Click the options on the main account, then "Add funds"
3. Add the balance you removed, listing your Collective as the source
